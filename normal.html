PREVENTION STRATEGIES

Break at least one deadlock condition.

Select a strategy above to watch its allocation rule in action.

01

N - 1 contenders

Limit the number of philosophers holding chopsticks to one fewer than the number at the table.

02

Resource ordering

Always request the lower-numbered chopstick before the higher-numbered one. Circular wait cannot form.

03

Arbitrator / waiter

A central waiter grants both chopsticks together only when both are available.

04

Asymmetric acquisition

Alternate acquisition order between philosophers so they do not all hold the same side first.
