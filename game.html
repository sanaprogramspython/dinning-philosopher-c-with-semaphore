SCORING

Think before you assign.

  • Complete an eating turn: +100
  • Survive each second: +1
  • Conflicting or invalid request: -10
  • Deadlock: -30

Thinking drains health slowly; hunger and waiting drain it faster. Eating restores health while both chopsticks stay held. Click an eating philosopher again to release both and finish the meal. During play, click a thinking philosopher to request both adjacent chopsticks at once. If either is occupied, no remote chopstick is taken. You can also request one hand at a time.
